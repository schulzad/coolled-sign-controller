# Brief

Draw a scene of your choice.

- **Canvas:** 384x384 pixels, one still image.
- **Colours:** only these eight, exactly: `#000000` black, `#ff0000` red, `#00ff00` green, `#0000ff` blue, `#00ffff` cyan, `#ff00ff` magenta, `#ffff00` yellow, `#ffffff` white. No other colours, and no transparency in the final.
- **Deliver:** `final.png` in your run folder, exactly 384x384 at one image pixel per canvas pixel, with the source you made it from beside it.
- **Check:** `python3 /Users/adamschulz/source/hacking/pixel-bakeoff/check.py final.png` must print `ok`.
- Take as long as you need, and stop when you'd be happy to sign it.
- Once you have finished, and not before, write `notes.md` in your run folder: what you made, how many times you looked at a render, and what you changed after looking.
